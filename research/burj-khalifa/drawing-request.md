# Burj Khalifa document request — UNSENT DRAFT

Suggested recipient: Emaar / Burj Khalifa document-control team. Ask them to identify the appropriate architectural, structural and façade document custodians rather than assuming that every contractor retains a releasable complete set.

Subject: Request for authorized Burj Khalifa technical reference drawings

We are assembling a source-linked architectural and construction study of Burj Khalifa for X-Ray. Please advise which drawings or technical excerpts can be released for this purpose, and the applicable conditions for local storage, tracing, visual reconstruction and publication.

The first priority is the drawing/document register, identifying discipline, sheet number, title, revision, issue date, status and superseded versions. Please identify whether each document is design intent, issued for construction, a shop drawing or an as-built record.

Requested packages, in priority order:

1. Survey datum, grid, site plan, tower elevations and complete storey/level schedule.
2. Foundation pile layout and schedule, pile/raft sections, geotechnical design summary, reinforcement and waterproofing details.
3. Representative and changing architectural/structural floor plates, core sections, wall/column schedules, slab edges and setback levels.
4. Upper steel and spire general arrangements, member schedules and erection sequence summaries.
5. Façade elevations and panel setting-out schedules; representative unitised-panel sections; glass types/build-ups/coating surfaces; mullion, transom, bracket, anchor, gasket, seal and movement-joint details; performance specifications and approved mock-up/test summaries.
6. Coordinated services/riser diagrams, plant and lift schedules, representative interior/finish packages and external works.
7. Construction milestone programme, applicable handover/as-built index and non-sensitive commissioning/O&M excerpts.

PDF with legible dimensions is useful initially. Where release is permitted, DWG/DXF/IFC or native models should include units, coordinate reference, origin and revision metadata. Redacted or representative excerpts are welcome if a complete package cannot be shared.

We will distinguish documented geometry from inferred reconstruction and will not describe a study model as construction-ready or use unverified measurements for procurement. Please identify any restrictions or attribution requirements and whether separate permission is needed from contributing designers or suppliers.

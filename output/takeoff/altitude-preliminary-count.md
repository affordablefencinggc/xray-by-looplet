## Deduplication and scope

Inspected all current floor-plan pages 42-53 visually. EDG 2 comparison insets, repeated text, legends and other massing options were excluded. Counted rooms once per current floor plan; repeated floors were multiplied only where their ranges are printed. Suites are included in the 209 hotel total and must not be added again. Artist lofts are separate.

These are typical-layout extrapolations, not a reconciled final accommodation schedule. Missing floor-specific plans for 7, 32 and 51 and conflicting narrative/level numbering prevent an asserted total building storey count. Basement parking page 42 says 3 typical levels and page 43 shows a different basement layout; no inferred total parking capacity is issued.

## Material quantities, volume and weight

No reliable full-building window/door or structural fastener schedule is supplied in this early-design package. Steel, concrete, reinforcement and MEP quantities remain uncounted. Room counts cannot establish a procurement list.

Packed/stacked storage volume (m3) and specified weight (kg): **unknown**, not zero. Actual material quantities and package dimensions are required; use specified unit or package weights where provided. Aisle/handling allowance remains separate.

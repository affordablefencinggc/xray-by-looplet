# Altitude materials and storage

With the Altitude drawing selected, open Components, then Materials & storage. Add each actual schedule or order line with a unique stock code, description, quantity, unit and source reference. Select an existing line to edit it. Saves persist on this browser and project; Export materials CSV creates a portable copy of the entered list and calculations.

Supported quantities: individual items, metres, square metres, cubic metres and kilograms. Missing quantities, dimensions and weights remain unknown. The coverage figures show how many entered lines can be calculated; they do not certify a complete building stock list.

Storage volume is the number of whole packages needed, rounded up, multiplied by their supplied outer length, width and height in metres. This is packed volume in cubic metres. It excludes aisles, handling clearances and stacking restrictions.

Specified weight can apply to one stock unit or one full package. Unit weight multiplies the stock quantity. Full-package weight multiplies the rounded package count, so the last partly filled package may weigh less than that estimate. A stock quantity already measured in kilograms supplies its unit-based weight directly. No material density is assumed.

Existing saved drawing counts, notes and reviews are retained when adding materials. Unreadable or newer saved versions remain protected from ordinary saves. A rejected write leaves the saved snapshot intact and keeps the form available for retry.

The Altitude PDF is early design guidance dated 15 December 2015. It does not establish an actual material order, verified window schedule, structural bill of materials or supplier packaging. Its provisional 252 residential and 209 hotel entrance allowances are separate from actual stock. The register starts empty. All QA screenshots with material quantities are explicitly fictional test data.

Further source review of the elevation/context studies on pages 9, 18 and 19 did not establish new construction quantities. Neighboring buildings and context drawings must not be included in Altitude totals. Actual whole-building stock volume, specified weight and a source-derived 3D reconstruction remain outstanding.

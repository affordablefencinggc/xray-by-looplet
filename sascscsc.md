  1. Parametric Architectural Elements (Smart Objects, Not Dumb Lines)
Draftsmen don't draw raw lines for buildings; they place "hosted" architectural assemblies:

Composite Walls (Assemblies): Walls with real layers (e.g., 110mm Brickwork + 50mm Cavity + 90mm Stud + 10mm Plasterboard).
Must have: Automatic clean corner T-junctions, mitering, and wall-join healing.
Hosted Openings (Doors & Windows):
When a door/window is dropped onto a wall, it must automatically cut the opening. If moved or deleted, the wall must heal itself.
Configurable swing directions (in/out, left/right), sill heights, and head heights.
Parametric Roofs:
Define roof footprints with individual edge pitch controls (instant hip, gable, skillion/shed, or flat).
Auto-generation of fascia, gutters, and eave overhangs.
Slabs & Levels: Floor boundary sketching with step-down offsets (e.g., wet areas -50mm, garage -100mm).

Grid lines with alphanumeric bubbles (A, B, C / 1, 2, 3).

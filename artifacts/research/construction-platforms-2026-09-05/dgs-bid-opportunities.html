Bid Opportunities

View bid opportunities to do business with the Department of General Services

Vendors seeking to do business with the Department of General Services (DGS) can view current bid opportunities on the California State Contracts Register (CSCR).

To search for DGS opportunities enter 7760 in the “Department” field. 

Contracts for services, public works and architecture and engineering work are initiated through the Office of Business and Acquisition Services.

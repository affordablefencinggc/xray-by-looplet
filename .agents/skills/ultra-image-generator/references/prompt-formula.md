# The 5-Part Ultra-Realistic Architectural Prompt Formula

This reference details the exact prompt synthesis pipeline required to bypass standard synthetic/CGI rendering outputs and produce editorial-grade, monograph-standard architectural photography.

---

## The Core Problem with Standard AI Renders

When asked to render buildings, standard AI image models default to a "video game / real-time CGI engine" aesthetic characterized by:
1. **Razor-sharp computer edges** with zero bevels or real-world tolerances.
2. **Repetitive tiled textures** with zero weathering, mortar thickness variation, or firing differentials.
3. **Synthetic billboard foliage** with identical leaves, unnatural neon-green grass, and cloned trees.
4. **Hollow or pitch-black windows** with flat reflections rather than authentic double-glazed refraction and subtle interior life.
5. **Over-saturated dynamic range** with extreme artificial contrast and plastic specular highlights.

---

## The 5-Part Formula Breakdown

### 1. Camera Hardware & Optical Physics
Force physical medium-format camera optics and architectural perspective control:
* **Camera Body:** `Medium format Hasselblad H6D-100c` or `Fujifilm GFX 100 II`.
* **Lens:** `35mm tilt-shift architectural lens, f/8 aperture`.
* **Perspective Control:** `Two-point architectural perspective, perfectly straight vertical lines, zero keystoning distortion`.
* **Depth of Field:** `Edge-to-edge f/8 architectural sharpness, deep depth of field across the entire structure`.

### 2. Explicit Anti-CGI Directives
Set explicit negative-style guardrails embedded in positive descriptive language:
* *"A completely genuine, indistinguishable from reality, raw photograph published in an architectural monograph."*
* *"Zero CGI feel, no 3D rendering artifacts, no video game gloss, no plastic sheen, no digital over-sharpening."*
* *"True-to-life dynamic range and natural chromatic balance."*

### 3. Construction Details & Material Tactility (Micro-Imperfections)
Describe the physical joinery, fastenings, and material assembly:
* **Roofing:** `Pitched corrugated Colorbond metal roofing in [Color, e.g. shale grey / monument] with realistic sheet lap seams, ridge capping, fascia, gutters, downpipe connections, and flush-mounted solar panel rails.`
* **Masonry / Cladding:** `Kiln-fired brickwork with subtle natural tone variance, tactile mortar beds, and chisel-dressed sandstone corner quoins [or board-formed concrete with tie-rod holes].`
* **Glazing & Windows:** `Dark charcoal powder-coated aluminium frames with double-glazed glass exhibiting authentic optical exterior reflections of the sky and surroundings, alongside subtle interior depth and room lighting.`
* **Ground / Driveway:** `Genuine exposed pebble aggregate concrete paving with saw-cut expansion joints, natural edge transitions, and realistic surface wear.`

### 4. Botanical & Environmental Authenticity
Never use generic terms like "trees" or "grass". Name specific regional botany and topography:
* **Native Trees:** `Natural hillside populated with authentic mature Australian eucalyptus / gum trees featuring organic branch structures, peeling bark, and natural canopy translucency.`
* **Groundcover & Garden:** `Natural turf lawn with blade density and subtle thatch variation; landscaped garden beds with organic mulch, flowering purple salvia, and native tussock grasses.`
* **Site Works:** `Board-formed concrete or stacked stone retaining walls holding back the natural earth slope.`

### 5. Natural Daylight & Atmospheric Physics
Model the physics of sunlight and environmental bounce:
* **Sun Angle:** `Soft natural late-afternoon golden-hour directional daylight.`
* **Shadows & Bounce:** `Realistic soft shadow falloff under eaves and carports, authentic ambient bounce light from the driveway illuminating undercroft ceilings, subtle recessed downlight warmth.`
* **Exposure:** `Balanced highlight roll-off without synthetic HDR halos, realistic atmospheric depth.`

## Assembly Template

```text
A completely genuine, indistinguishable from reality, raw photograph of [BUILDING TYPOLOGY & LAYOUT], published in an architectural monograph. 
Shot on a medium format Hasselblad H6D-100c camera, 35mm tilt-shift architectural lens, f/8, natural light. Zero CGI feel, no 3D rendering artifacts, no video game gloss. 

[LOCATION / SETTING CONTEXT]: the [NUMBER]-storey structure features [DISTINCTIVE GEOMETRIES: e.g. deep cantilevered carport, clean roofline, expansive glazing]. 

Materials: [SPECIFY EXACT MASONRY / CLADDING / ROOFING / FRAMING DETAILS].
Ground & Access: [SPECIFY DRIVEWAY / PATH / PAVING / EXPANSION JOINTS].
Glazing: [SPECIFY FRAME FINISH, DOUBLE-GLAZING REFLECTIONS, INTERIOR DEPTH].
Landscape & Site: [SPECIFY REGIONAL SPECIES, BARK, TURF, TOPOGRAPHY, RETAINING WALLS].

Lighting: Soft natural directional daylight with realistic ambient ground bounce, authentic shadow falloff, perfectly balanced exposure, natural chromatic balance, and true-to-life dynamic range.
```

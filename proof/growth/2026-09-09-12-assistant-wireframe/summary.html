What changed

The Live assistant can now turn 'draw me a 3-storey 20 m by 12 m wireframe' into real design geometry: draw_architect_elements accepts level, slab, roof, footprint and extrude operations (append-only, validated, stable IDs) and two new read tools explain X-Ray's data model and the source-building reconstruction so the model can reason about the structure before drawing. With the real provider, one message produced three levels, twelve walls, three slabs and a hip roof at 20000 × 12000 mm, saved and restored after reload; a second message added a storey and undid it through the undo tool. Separately, the repository's ledger, X-Ray engine and Fast CDP skills are now enforced by Claude Code hooks that apply to any selected model: whole-tree staging is denied, git history mutations and process kills are gated, the verified-build pointer is guarded, and a completion claim without proof is blocked.

04 The four dependencies that gate the most

All 19 blocked rows reduce to four missing external things. Three of them are one decision each.

12 rows No account service

Blocks all of category A (administration, accounts, access) and much of V and Z — sign-in, roles, permissions, audit, organisation boundaries, activity logs, security and tenant isolation.

"No account service is selected, which blocks all of category A and much of V and Z." Nothing ships that pretends otherwise: authentication is off by default and the account shell does not show a dev fallback identity.

3 rows Live search credential

P-01 user-initiated search, P-03 structured product extraction and X-09 pricing research all need a configured search credential. The honest-failure branch is proven live (503 not-configured); real results are only proven against a mocked provider.

3 rows Authenticated transport

V-05 recipients and distribution, V-06 transmittals and receipts, V-07 download links and expiry. "Nothing is sent, so no durable receipt exists."

1 row Third-party connectors

I-11 needs connectors with user-granted permissions. Provider credentials today are server-side environment values with an operator flag — not per-user tokens — so there is no subject to test revocation against.

05 Delivery order

The register names these as implementation waves, not a narrowing of the catalogue. All categories stay in scope; dependencies decide the order. The parallelism is the plan's own assignment table.

  1. Close the known gaps

    Residential infill and before-state contract · roofing boundary explanation and an independent review correction · QS CSV explanation and tablet acceptance. Coordinator reconciles stale status records and defines the shared evidence contract.

  2. Complete geometry and source binding

    Residential authored infill and changed-repair geometry · roofing measured planes, edges and penetrations · HVAC section and network identities with explicit fittings. Integrate shared source references, persistence and revision invalidation.

  3. Quantities and schedules

    Residential lifecycle allocation and demolition/repair schedules · roofing stock layout, flashing and fixing schedules · HVAC coordinated network and equipment schedules · QS rate-book binding, alternatives and revision comparison.

  4. End-to-end acceptance

    Residential coordinated issue workflow · roofing job walkthrough · HVAC job walkthrough, then the QS slot. Shared regression, production and native checks, independent acceptance review, per-industry evidence consolidated.

  5. One project lifecycle

    The register's own step 2: stable project, document and sheet IDs, a full backup asset manifest, save feedback, reopen, archive/restore, and concurrent-edit protection. This is the prerequisite for B, D and V together.

  6. Documents, sheets and pricing

    Complete named sheet sets with safe sheet removal and recovery · rate books, worksheet column mapping and reviewed application. Costs must not reinterpret measured quantities.

  7. Accounts, handoff and delivery

    Portable staff handoff first, then authenticated team storage, permissions, invitations and durable delivery receipts — the account-service decision above, taken in order.

  8. Discipline engines

    Specialist solvers, standards content, hardware formats and proprietary integrations each need a separately validated engine or licensed adapter. Producing geometry or a quantity never constitutes an engineering solution.

The honest half of the plan. Eleven whole categories are gaps because X-Ray holds discipline labels rather than engineering engines: geospatial, services, programme, libraries, landscape, manufacturing, electrical, operations, structural, whole-life and specialist assets. Naming a trade in a takeoff category never constitutes a solver for it.

The register's list of thirteen whole-gap categories predates SO-01 and PH-01 landing as partial rows, so it now reads eleven — the same drift as the header table, in the honest direction.

06 What today added

The live assistant was used as its own test harness on an isolated origin, driving real MiniMax-M3 turns through the shipped UI. Seven scenarios, one turn each. No application source changed — this is evidence, not a fix.

Six scenarios pass

Tool execution reported the real receipt, including the nine-decimal boundary value rather than a rounded one, and said plainly that per-sheet waste and offcuts are not calculated. A failed calculation was reported as failed with no substituted answer. A discussion-only request produced no tool calls. A reload at tablet size returned the conversation byte-identical. Switching projects gave a separate, empty conversation, and switching back restored the first exactly.

One real defect

An objective that forbids tools but is not classified as discussion-only — No tools. Reply with exactly: LIVE TURN OK — delivers nothing. The app strips every tool declaration, the completion router still demands a step, and after two corrections it discards the answer it already has. The user sees a red banner instead of the two words they asked for.

The combination has no test: the correction limit is covered only with tools available. Reproduced once on a verified-empty conversation, never retried for a nicer answer.

A harness defect, recorded

The first sweep was invalid and is preserved but excluded. The CDP profile persists, so the poller matched a previous run's answer within seconds. Fixed by baselining the entry count before each send and starting every scenario through the app's own New chat control. Any live-assistant evidence that predates this fix should be re-read with that in mind.

Verified, not assumed

The internal withheld-candidate marker is not visible to users — checked in the rendered panel and in the page's own text before and after a reload, rather than inferred from the code.

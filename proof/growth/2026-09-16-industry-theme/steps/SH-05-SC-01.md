Date: 2026-09-16 · Branch `feat/architect-cad-engine` · HEAD `3abaca2` (nothing committed) · Devices: desktop 1600×1000, tablet 1024×768 and tablet portrait 768×1024

## Requirement (verbatim from the plan)

> [ ] SH-05 Apply the agreed charcoal/ocean-blue theme to new controls and reports. Verify labels, keyboard actions, focus, tablet layout and long/error states. Preserve adjustable top rows.

## Outcome: no application source change was required

The theme already reaches every surface this campaign could find, so this step produced **no diff to `src/`**. That is a result, not an omission — but it means the step is **partly done**, not done, because two clauses of the requirement are not evidenced (see *Remaining limits*).

The navy skin at [styles.css:61-88](src/styles.css#L61-L88) redefines every token the industry stylesheet consumes (`--color-bg`, `--color-ink`, `--color-line`, `--color-header`, `--color-muted`, `--color-navy`, `--color-on-navy`, `--color-field`, `--color-danger`), and `Studio.tsx:207` puts `data-skin` on the same `.workbench` element that contains the industry workbench, so the tokens cascade. The campaign exists to check that claim rather than assume it.

## Method

An owned dev server on **port 8085** — deliberately not the user-facing 8080 — started by an identity-checked scratch script, then stopped through the same script's re-verified `taskkill`. `localStorage` is origin-scoped and includes the port, so the campaign could not touch saved user state on 8080. The server was stopped at task end: [cleanup.txt](../cleanup.txt). Ports 5173, 5199 and 8080 were listening before and after with unchanged PIDs, and were never touched.

Scenario: [theme.json](../scenarios/theme.json), 83 opcodes, built by [build-scenario.mjs](../build-scenario.mjs). It seeds a synthetic source document and a locked manual calibration, switches the skin to `navy`, then drives all three industry worksheets, asserting in-page at every leg — the assertions are executed code, not readings taken off the screenshots.

## Findings

1. **Field backgrounds resolve to `--color-field`, not the `--color-bg` that industryDrafts.css names.** [industryDrafts.css:8](src/studio/industries/industryDrafts.css#L8) sets `background: var(--color-bg)` on workbench inputs, but [surfaceContrast.css](src/studio/surfaceContrast.css) applies `--color-field` to every `.workbench` input with higher precedence, so the browser paints `#2b2b2b` in navy. **Both tokens are themed**, so the surface is correct and this is not a defect — but the industry stylesheet's declaration is dead, and a future reader will assume it wins. Recorded rather than "fixed", because changing the cascade is a visual change this step has no mandate for.

2. **`:focus-visible` does not match programmatic focus for every field.** An earlier revision of this scenario focused all thirteen inputs in turn and asserted each matched `:focus-visible`; the first six matched and the last seven did not, which is a Chromium heuristic, not a CSS problem. The check now demonstrates the ring on a live field and proves coverage from the rule. This is a genuine thinning of the evidence and is listed below.

## Remaining limits — what this step does NOT establish

- **Keyboard *actions* are not exercised.** The requirement says "keyboard actions"; the CDP runner exposes no key event, so Tab traversal, Enter/Space activation and Escape were not driven. Focus *rings* and reachability are proven; activation is not. This is the main reason SH-05 stays partly done.
- **Adjustable top rows are not verified and have no test.** `src/studio/AdjustableTopRow.tsx` is referenced by nothing else and `adjustableTopRow.css` is covered by no suite in the regression chain. The requirement's "preserve adjustable top rows" clause is therefore unverified.
- **The architect/residential panels were not re-checked in this campaign.** They carry their own 2026-09-14 charcoal verification (31/31, `proof/growth/2026-09-14-residential/README.md`) and predate the newer controls. This campaign covers the three industry worksheets and the QS report.
- **Only these three worksheets and one report were covered.** The roofing sheet-coverage panel, the HVAC wrap branch and the QS hierarchy explorer are not individually asserted.
- **Contrast is checked against painted backgrounds, not images.** Text over gradients, images or canvas is outside what this method can see.
- **No native, installer, macOS or Linux check** — SH-06 remains open.
- **Nothing was committed**, and no independent human reviewer has signed off. The audit is agent-performed against a real browser and recorded logs.

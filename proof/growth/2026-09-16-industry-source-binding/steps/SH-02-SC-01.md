## Rules the tests establish

1. Identity is never silently normalised: padded `projectId`/`sourceRevisionId`, a short or uppercase hash, an unknown unit or evidence class, a timezone-free timestamp, a fractional or negative page index, and unknown keys are all refused. Free user wording (`reference`) is trimmed, and the stored value is asserted so the change is visible.
2. A `page`/`model` binding whose evidence is `traced`, `dimensioned` or `inferred` **must** carry a calibration; a `document` locator may not carry one; a `declared` binding may not carry one. A typed reference therefore cannot masquerade as a calibrated measurement.
3. Editing the bound revision (`source-revised`), swapping it (`source-replaced`), deleting it (`source-missing`), switching project (`project-changed`) or re-locking the calibration (`calibration-changed`) each make the binding stale, and `requireCurrentIndustryBinding` refuses a stale binding naming every reason in a stable order.
4. Evaluation does not rewrite the stored binding (asserted by deep-equality before and after).
5. Only `traced` and `dimensioned` count as source-verified; `declared` and `inferred` never do — so no worksheet can use this helper to claim verification.
6. A calibration's identity is its graded content (`sheet`, `source`, `metresPerUnit`, selected candidate), so re-locking the same sheet at a new scale invalidates a binding made against the old one — an unlocked calibration has no identity at all.
7. A document with a null `sha256` cannot anchor a binding, and only the active document's calibrations are offered; the missing pieces become `null`, which then reads as stale rather than as a remembered copy.
8. (Added 2026-09-16.) The source's display label is cosmetic and bounded: a document's own label is carried through for display, a blank one falls back to the revision id, and an overlong one is truncated at 240 characters rather than rejected by the contract. Identity remains the revision id and hash.

## Remaining limits

- No assistant tool argument consumes the binding yet; the contract is reachable from the worksheets, but not from the assistant.
- Only the active document/sheet is offered as a binding candidate. Choosing among several sources is not implemented.
- Invalidation is evaluated on read; there is no background re-evaluation while a worksheet sits closed and the project changes underneath it.
- No industry-readiness, quote-eligibility or native/desktop acceptance is claimed. `verifiedQuoteEligible` stays `false` everywhere in this work.

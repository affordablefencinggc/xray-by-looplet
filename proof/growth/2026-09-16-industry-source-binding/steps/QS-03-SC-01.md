## What the machine checks separately

`assertQsSaved` reads the saved draft and asserts the binding's `units`/`evidenceClass`/`sourceName`, that `form.calculated === true`, and that exactly one quantity row is assigned to a classification — the report the screenshot shows is the one the draft actually holds.

## Remaining limits

- The document and its locked calibration are seeded through the app's own restore path; the classification, the quantity, the binding and the calculation are driven through the real controls.
- The binding's own `sha256` is not asserted for this worksheet (it is for roofing and HVAC); the stale transition is proven by the withheld state and the reason text instead.
- Quantity evidence remains per-item ("unverified", "Unavailable — manual entry" for a manually entered row). Binding the worksheet does not promote an item to source-verified.
- No cost rate, price book or quote eligibility is claimed here.

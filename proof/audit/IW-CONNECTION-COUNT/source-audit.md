# Independent drawing trace — Thornton Fire Station 8

Source: City of Thornton structural bid set, 2024-09-27, 23 pages. Original PDF and SHA-256 are recorded in `artifacts/research/connection-count-2026-09-06/source-record.json`.

The original S2.2 plan (PDF page 10) was rendered and visually inspected at full-sheet and enlarged-bay resolutions. The selected span is the second main apparatus-bay span from the north, between W24×94 girders labelled 125′-0¾″ and 125′-4″. Ten north–south W10×22 lines appear across the span. This trial intentionally selects the **eight interior lines**, excluding the western and eastern outermost lines and all neighbouring spans.

Manual trace, west to east: I1, I2, I3, I4, I5, I6, I7, I8. Each has one north end and one south end. These are takeoff IDs, not printed structural marks. Sheet coordinates locate evidence only; no dimensions were scaled.

The typical connection callout is 1/S5.1 (PDF page 18). Its note uses the lesser nominal beam depth. W10×22 meeting W24×94 therefore uses D=10 inches. S0.6 (PDF page 6) gives **two 7/8-inch ASTM A325N bolts** for nominal depths 8–11 inches. The three generic detail views describe one physical connection and add no instances.

Independent arithmetic: 8 traced beam lines × 2 physical ends × 2 scheduled bolts = **32 bolts across 16 connections**. This is a preliminary application of a typical detail requiring connection-by-connection review, not a fabrication quantity or an engineer's approval. The unselected spans, outermost beam lines, bracing, girder ends, decks, stairs, anchors and other trades remain outside the count. The set's general notes require shop drawings/deferred submissions for other work.

Nut/washer specifications and quantities, bolt grip/length, specified hardware mass and packed volume have not been established by these three references and remain null. The 22 in W10×22 describes the beam designation, not a bolt weight. No whole-building or high-rise completeness claim is made.

Evidence crops are unmodified PDF renders: plan (.385,.452,.16,.091), schedule (.62,.427,.21,.32), detail (.665,.045,.211,.293), in normalized top-left page coordinates. Overlay markers belong to the application, not the underlying drawings.

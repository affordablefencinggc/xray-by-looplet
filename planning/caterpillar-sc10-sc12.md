# Caterpillar execution schedule — SC-10 through SC-12

Status: planning only. This schedule does not start or complete any slice.

The caterpillar advances one wave at a time. Within an admitted wave, the three lanes may run in parallel, but they own non-overlapping surfaces: Lane A owns contracts, schemas, domain rules, ADRs, and decision records; Lane B owns production implementation and UI; Lane C owns test fixtures, runners, proof records, screenshots, and evidence indexes. A lane may review another lane's diff but may not edit that lane's files. No subsequent wave starts until the current merge barrier passes.

SC-10 remains externally blocked until the named Looplet owners supply and approve the authoritative contract material. Planning, schemas, and rejected-input fixtures may be prepared, but no endpoint guessing, credential invention, network probing, external write, or adapter implementation can satisfy that gate. Every existing sidecar, installer, and packaged executable remains untrusted: it may be hashed or inventoried without execution, but only a same-run candidate rebuilt from reviewed source may execute after its provenance gate passes.

## Blocking decisions and non-negotiable gates

1. SC-10 cannot close without authoritative Looplet ownership, contract artifacts, sandbox observations, and joint signoff (`EXT-LOOPLET-01/02/03`, `EXT-ID-01`). No guessed integration is permitted.
2. SC-11B cannot start executable-candidate work until SC-07G BR-013 has current Unix process-group proof. Existing sidecars, `engine/bin/xray-engine.exe`, ignored sidecars, `public/X-Ray-by-Looplet-Setup.exe`, installers, and old `target` outputs remain read/inventory-only and must never execute.
3. U-04 freezes connected bootstrap versus fixed/offline WebView prerequisites before clean-machine testing; U-08 freezes uninstall/user-data retention; U-02/U-07 define platform and multi-job lifecycle; U-06 freezes the migration support floor; U-09 freezes any signing/encryption/password claim.
4. Clean-machine proof is valid only for the exact same-run reviewed and hash-approved installer in a disposable VM proven free of repository, Node, Rust, Python, old X-Ray installations, and undeclared runtime dependencies.
5. Packaged no-network continuity is mandatory for the approved core workflow. Web cold-offline remains a separate product decision and must resolve exactly one branch: intentional-off (`DC-077`) or approved shell caching (`DC-078`–`DC-081`).
6. Every wave is stopped by its merge barrier until its code or explicit disposition diff **and** its row-appropriate executed, human, browser, native, clean-machine, or external proof are attached. Parallel lanes never authorize a later wave to overtake an incomplete barrier.

# Caterpillar execution schedule: SC-13 through SC-16

Status: planning only. This schedule does not advance any slice.

The three lanes may work concurrently only inside one wave and only after its entry gate is satisfied. A later wave may begin only after the current wave's merge barrier is proved. Lane A owns contracts, domain models, ADRs, and policy. Lane B owns implementation and UI. Lane C owns independent tests, executed evidence, and human-proof manifests. No lane may edit another lane's owned surface during its wave; shared files are integrated once at the merge barrier by the slice integrator.

Auth and application database persistence remain **OFF** until SC-13 records explicit identity and storage decisions. An ON branch is not authorised by this schedule. No local build, workflow definition, mock, document, or unsigned artifact may be used as a substitute for remote CI, signing, sandbox, supported-platform, or clean-machine proof.

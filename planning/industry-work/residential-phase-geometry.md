# IND-01 next slice: before/proposed geometry

Design proposal from the parallel audit; not implemented or accepted. Lifecycle classification alone must not unlock alteration quantities or procurement.

Resolve a derived stage model before calling geometry functions. Keep the saved all-work model and frozen historical issues unchanged. Every output carries its stage, source/baseline reference and unresolved conditions.

| Classification | Before | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing | Present | Present |
| New | Absent | Present |
| Demolished | Present | Absent |
| Repaired | Present | Present; repair scope remains separately explicit |
| Unassigned | Undetermined | Undetermined; blocks complete totals |

Existing/repaired geometry needs an explicit unchanged-across-stages declaration or a compatible frozen baseline issue. A later edit cannot silently rewrite the before state.

Opening disposition is separate from removing a door/window. A demolished opening on a retained wall requires explicit retain-void or infill intent. Infill must reference authored physical geometry and its layers; never invent a wall assembly. A new opening cuts the retained wall only in the proposed stage. Its geometric delta is not automatically a priced strip-out or disposal quantity.

Run wall junction clipping and room detection independently per stage. Cross-category shared volume remains an unresolved allocation until a reviewed ownership rule exists; lexical element IDs cannot determine claimed new/retained quantities. Keep the material-sync guard until these conditions are supported and proven.

Acceptance fixtures:

- Coincident demolished/new 4 m x 2.7 m x 0.2 m walls: 2.16 m3 each stage, independent of IDs.
- Retained wall with new 0.9 m x 2.1 m opening: 2.16 m3 before, 1.782 m3 proposed, 0.378 m3 geometric delta only.
- Demolished door in retained wall: missing disposition blocks proposed quantities; retain-void preserves the cut.
- Explicit infill restores only its own authored solid volume/material identity.
- Demolished host and its openings leave proposed geometry; contradictory new hosted opening flags a blocker.
- Perpendicular retained/new junction: stage union correct; unresolved shared allocation disclosed and invariant under ID swaps.
- Unassigned legacy geometry prevents a complete total without mutating saved bytes.
- Repair with changed geometry requires a before-state basis; historical baseline remains frozen.
- Before/proposed room boundaries reflect wall removal.
- Screenshots, exports, undo, reload and issued history retain exact stage/baseline identity.

Classification, Before alteration and Proposed must be separate labelled views, with status legend and unresolved-condition notices on screen and exported sheets. Disposal/salvage/hazard allowances and repair rates remain later explicit work under PH-02.
